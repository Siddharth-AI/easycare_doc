📝 Is screen me kya hota hai

  • Ek product ki poori details ek hi form me: naam, company, category/sub-category, disease/pest (kis ke liye), photos, packaging, price, points, crops, description.
  • QR code + barcode + product code apne-aap ban jaate (scan/track ke liye).
  • Photos me ek ko "MAIN" mark karte — wahi product ki main image dikhegi.
  • Pricing me MRP, user ka selling price, GST, aur har level (franchise) ka rate. Ye rate har product ke apne hote hain — system me fix nahi.
  • Billing ke waqt cost price change ki ja sakti hai — bill usi cost pe banega. Yahan wali cost sirf default hai.
  • Crops me ek product ko kai fasal ke liye tag kar sakte (Wheat, Cotton…).
  • Save dabate hi product ban jaata, aur uska code apne-aap ban jaata (category code + naam).

📝 Is screen me kya hota hai

  • Stock batch me aata hai — har batch ka number, banne ki date aur expiry date.
  • Hisaab simple: Pichla + Aaya = Total, phir jitna bika utna minus → Bacha.
  • Example: pehle 100 aaya (pichla 0 → total 100), usme se 10 bika → 90 bacha. Phir 30 aur aaya (pichla 90 → total 120), usme se 50 bika → 70 bacha.
  • Total available abhi = 70 — yahi stock product list me dikhta.
  • Bechte waqt jiski expiry pehle wo batch pehle jaata (kuch kharab na ho).
  • Stock kam ho to system alert deta — reorder ke liye.
