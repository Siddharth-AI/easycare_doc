02 · System Ek Nazar Me

Do structure, saath-saath chalte hain

Inko alag samajhna sabse zaroori hai — baaki poora system isi farq pe khada hai.

Franchise Network

Kya hai: aapki physical supply chain — warehouse, mini-warehouse, franchise shops.

Shape: WH → MWH (optional) → L1. L2 aur L3 dono seedha L1 ke neeche — aapas me bhai-bhai, ek doosre ke neeche nahi.

Kaam: stock bhejna, paisa manage karna, credit aur stock limit.

Kamai: product margin (price ladder) + commission.

Member Network

Kya hai: aapki selling community — members (BPA) jo kharidte aur refer karte hain.

Shape: binary tree — har member ke Left aur Right, do leg.

Kaam: kisne kisko laaya, aur incentive calculate karna.

Kamai: points (PV) se incentive.

03 · Module 1–3 · Franchise, Product, Billing

Ek product, WH se member tak

3.1 Network aur service area

🟪 Rule — Network (badla hua)

Upar ek Warehouse (WH) — saara stock wahin se. Mini-Warehouse optional — kahin hoga, kahin nahi, ek se zyada bhi ho sakte. Uske neeche Level 1, aur L1 ke neeche seedha Level 2 aur Level 3 dono.

Ye pehle se badla hai: pehle chain seedhi thi — L1 → L2 → L3 (L3, L2 ke neeche). Ab L2 aur L3 dono L1 ke direct neeche hain — aapas me bhai-bhai, ek doosre ke neeche nahi.

🟩 Confirmed — GST

Har product pe apna GST % set hota hai (example: 10%), aur split hamesha CGST 5% + SGST 5% — automatic bill pe.

Inventory se bill banate waqt cost price change ki ja sakti hai. Bill usi customize ki hui cost pe banega — product master ka default cost sirf shuruaati value hai, patthar ki lakeer nahi. Har bill me kaunsi cost use hui, wo record me save rehti hai.

3.3 Price ladder — har level ka alag price

Product ki price sirf ek hi nahi hoti — har franchise level ka apna price hota hai (neeche dekhiye), aur yehi ladder commission ka base hai.

Ye ladder poore system ke liye hardcode nahi hai. Har product ke apne level-price hote hain — Admin product master me MWH / L1 / L2 / L3 ka rate aur selling price set karta hai, aur commission usi product ke rate se nikalta hai. 🟪 Rule — L2 aur L3 alag-alag raste hain

Ab L2 aur L3 dono L1 ke neeche hain, isliye ek hi chain me teeno kabhi nahi aate. Customer jis shop se khareedta hai, chain wahin khatam ho jaati hai — L2 wala raasta ya L3 wala raasta.

🟩 Confirmed — Commission kaise bantega

L2 aur L3 ke beech ab koi rishta nahi hai. Baaki flow bilkul pehle jaisa hi chalega — har level ko apna set commission milta hai.

Sale L2 se hui → L1 aur L2 ko apna-apna commission. Sale L3 se hui → L1 aur L3 ko apna-apna. Ek raaste ka level doosre raaste me aata hi nahi, isliye aapas me kuch bantta bhi nahi.

Aur commission ka number product master se aata hai — Admin har product pe har level ka rate set karta hai. System me koi fix ladder hardcode nahi hai.

🟩 Confirmed — "Missing level ka roll-up" rule KHATAM

Purana rule tha: koi level chain me na ho to uska commission upar wale ko mil jaata tha. Ab ye rule poori tarah khatam hai. L2 aur L3 alag branch hain — L2 ka na hona L3 ko affect hi nahi karta, aur ulta bhi. Beech me koi "gap" ban hi nahi sakta.

3.4 Billing, wallet aur stock — L1 aur L2/L3 ka flow ALAG hai

🟥 Rule — Do Alag Model, Kabhi Mix Nahi

L1 = POSTPAID (pehle maal, baad me paisa) aur L2 / L3 = PREPAID (pehle paisa, baad me maal). Ye do bilkul alag flow hain — inka billing, wallet aur limit ka behaviour ek jaisa nahi hai, aur inhe kabhi mila kar nahi chalaya jaayega.

L1 — Postpaid (credit limit)

L1 ko ek fix limit milti hai (example: ₹15,00,000). Us limit tak wo bina paisa diye maal utha sakta hai — pehle stock, payment baad me.

Limit khatam hone pe aage maal nahi milega jab tak payment na ho jaaye.

Limit har franchise ki apni hoti hai — Admin har L1 ke liye alag set karta hai. L1 kabhi real money pehle nahi deta; uska wallet minus aur plus hota rehta hai.

L2 / L3 — Prepaid (wallet recharge)

L2 aur L3 ko pehle wallet me paisa daalna padta hai (recharge). Usi paise se maal milta hai — udhaar nahi.

Billing ka paisa unke wallet me jama hota hai — aur wahi unki den-dari hai (jo upar dena hai). Commission usi me adjust ho jaata hai.

🟪 Rule — Paisa Kaise Upar Jaata Hai

Jab L2/L3 ko MWH ya WH ko paisa dena hota hai, wo wallet-to-wallet transfer se jaata hai. Commission wallet ka paisa bhi isi me adjust ho jaata hai — cash haath se nahi chalta.

Margin split

Mini-WH → L1/L2/L3/customer = 50/50. L1 → customer = 50/50. L1 → L2 ya L1 → L3 (chain me neeche) = price ladder se, 50/50 nahi.

Commission rule

L2/L3 ka commission stock approve hote hi ban jaata hai. Paisa hamesha Warehouse ke saath settle hota — franchise aapas me cash nahi dete, wallet-to-wallet hota hai.

🟩 Confirmed — L1 kabhi real money nahi deta

L1 postpaid hai — wo pehle se paisa deta hi nahi. Uske paas ek limit hoti hai, aur uska wallet minus aur plus hota rehta hai — bilkul jaise pehle chal raha tha. Koi naya behaviour nahi.

Sirf L2/L3 ke liye naya hai: wahan pehle recharge karna padta hai, aur billing ka paisa wallet me jama hota hai (den-dari).

Poora flow reverse ho jaata hai. Jo stock gaya tha wo wapas, jo commission bana tha wo wapas — sab kuch ulta chal jaata hai. Wallet, stock aur commission teeno apne aap pehle wali position pe aa jaate hain, aur reverse ka bhi record rehta hai.

04 · Module 4 · Member Network (Binary Tree)

Har member ke do leg

Left (Team 1) aur Right (Team 2). Tree infinite neeche ja sakta hai.

🟩 Confirmed — Sponsor vs Placement

Ye dono alag cheezein hain, lekin poori tarah unrelated nahi. Jisne aapko laaya (sponsor), zaroori nahi wahi aapke theek upar ho (seedha parent) — par wo hamesha aapke kisi na kisi upar wale (upline) me hoga hi. Kyun? Kyunki naya member hamesha sponsor ke apne hi neeche chain-drill hokar lagta hai (Left ya Right) — kisi aur ki branch me kabhi nahi ja sakta.

Isliye: sponsor seedha parent na ho, ye ho sakta hai — par sponsor kabhi bhi "bahar ka" ya "bhai/cousin branch ka" nahi ho sakta. Wo hamesha aapki hi seedhi upar ki line me milega, chahe kitna bhi upar ho.

Chain rule — naya member kahan lagega

🟩 Rule — Left ka Left, Right ka Right

Sponsor se seedhi chain drill hoti hai — Left chuna to left → left → left… jab tak khaali slot na mile; Right chuna to right → right → right…. Chain na beech me rukti hai, na side badalti hai. Naya member hamesha sponsor ki neeche wali chain me lagta hai — idhar-udhar kisi aur branch me kabhi nahi.

Neeche buttons dabaiye — dekhiye ki right waali chain kabhi left me nahi mudti, aur left waali kabhi right me nahi.

Point flow — ek purchase se kya hota hai

Member U8 ne 1,000 PV ka product khareeda. U8 ko U1 ne sponsor kiya tha, par U8 tree me U6 ke left me baitha hai.

🟩 Confirmed Rules

Buyer ko sirf Self point milta hai — team point nahi. Sponsor ko Direct AUR Team point dono milte hain (agar wo upar bhi hai to). Team point Left ya Right bucket me jaata hai — depend karta hai buyer ki branch us upar-wale ki kaunsi side se neeche hai.

🟩 Confirmed — Carry Forward aur Washout ka poora rule

1 · Matching pehle AAJ ka business kharch karti hai, uske baad purana carry. Ye order sabse zaroori hai — isi se pura hisaab badal jaata hai.

2 · Purana carry jo bacha → hamesha aage jaata hai. Old carry kabhi wash nahi hota.

3 · Aaj ke business ka jo bacha → wahi wash hota hai.

4 · Strong side / Weak side — TOTAL se decide hota hai: dono side ka total (purana carry + aaj ka business) jodo — jiska total bada wo STRONG, jiska total chhota wo WEAK. Sirf aaj ke business se ya sirf carry se nahi — dono jod kar.

5 · Weak side pe washout nahi hua → strong side pe bhi nahi hoga. Uska bacha hua bhi carry forward ho jaayega.

6 · Matching bani hi nahi (1,000 se kam ya cap 0) → kuch wash nahi, dono side ka pura PV aage.

5.4 Startup, Mentor's aur Achievers

Startup

Direct PV milestones. 3,000 PV → ₹2,100 · 10,000 PV → +₹3,000 (total ₹5,100 = 51%). Ek hi baar milta hai — aur ab iski time limit bhi hai.

Mentor's

Sponsor tree (binary nahi) — 5 level, har level 7% of BPI = max 35%. Koi capping nahi.

Achievers

Org-1 + Org-2 PV + monthly direct sales. Rank + tour + company turnover ka %.

🟥 Mentor's — Chain Toot Jaati Hai

Agar aapke sponsee ke neeche kisi ko kisi aur ne sponsor kiya, to wo aapki level me nahi aata. Sirf wahi chain ginti hai jo sach me aapki sponsorship se neeche bani ho.

🟩 Confirmed — Startup Incentive pe TIME LIMIT

Startup Incentive ab hamesha ke liye khula nahi rahega. Member ki ID activation ki date se ek window chalti hai — Admin us window ko mahino me ya dino me set kar sakta hai (example: 180 din / 6 mahine).

Us window ke andar milestone achieve ho gaya to incentive milega. Window khatam hone ke baad Startup Incentive ban hi nahi sakta — member is scheme se bahar ho jaata hai. Ye limit pehle milestone pe lagti hai.

Window ki value poori tarah Admin ke haath me hai — jitne din ya mahine chahe rakh sakta hai, aur baad me badal bhi sakta hai. System me koi fix number hardcode nahi hoga. (180 din sirf ek example hai.)
